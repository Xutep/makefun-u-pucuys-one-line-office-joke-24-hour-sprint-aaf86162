`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, deterministic) writes one
single-line joke about office life — at most 200 characters — to
`/output/entry.txt`:

> This meeting could have been an email, but the email would have needed a meeting, so here we are billing the company for our own confusion.

Run it with `python3 main.py`.
